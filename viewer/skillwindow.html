Skill by time window

Step 1 of RiftsClimate's ladder, the plain Molchan test, one factor at a time, in the window you choose. Each factor rates the continental area of every time slice in the window, in the direction stated before any test. Each deposit in the window is ranked against that ground: its share is the share of the ground (area x time) rated higher than the place where it formed. Skill is 0.5 minus the average share: 0 is no better than placing deposits at random, positive means deposits sit on ground the factor rates highly. Nothing is fitted or chosen here.

Half the track is 0.4 of skill. The 5% level is the textbook one for a rating fixed in advance, 1.645 / sqrt(12 n) (Zechar & Jordan 2008), with n the districts in the window (deposits within 200 km and 30 Myr of another, joined in chains): deposits of one district share a setting, so they are not independent. Counting deposits instead gives a narrower level (shown above the list). A skill inside the band is what placing the deposits at random could give. Click a factor for its Molchan diagram and its skill through time. Over 0-1000 Ma these numbers differ slightly from the main test's step 1, which merges time slices in pairs to save time.
The Molchan diagram: searching the window's ground from the best rated down, how many deposits are still missed. The diagonal is random placement; the shaded area between the curve and the diagonal is the skill. Reversing the direction mirrors the curve and changes only the skill's sign.
